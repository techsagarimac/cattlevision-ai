# Future Scope

1. **Custom cattle detector** trained on local breed photographs (Holstein, Gir, Sahiwal, etc.).
2. **Multi-object tracking upgrades** (ByteTrack / DeepSORT) for crowded sheds.
3. **Behaviour dataset** with expert labels for lying, feeding, lame gait, and isolation.
4. **Sequence models** (LSTM / transformer) on track histories instead of rule thresholds.
5. **Multi-camera topology** mapping pens to camera IDs.
6. **Edge deployment** on a small farm PC or NVIDIA Jetson.
7. **Sensor fusion** with RFID, pedometers, and barn climate IoT.
8. **Cloud dashboard** with authentication, farms, and roles.
9. **Mobile client** for push alerts and photo capture in the field.
10. **Welfare research protocol** with a veterinary college for validation — still not a diagnostic claim.

The present repository is the MVP foundation: detection, tracking, rules, persistence, and a presentation-ready UI.
